# MCQ 第 11 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/XDSM-2027/XDSM-2027-19-Q11_题目.png|题目]]

$$r\left(\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{4}\right)=r\left(\alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{4}\right)=3 \neq r\left(\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}\right)=2,$$

即 $\alpha_{4}$ 不能由 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ 线性表示。

已知 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{4}$ 是 3 维非零列向量，则下列命题中：

(1) 若 $\alpha_{4}$ 可由 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ 线性表示，则 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3} + \alpha_{4}$ 线性无关；

(2) 若 $\alpha_{4}$ 可由 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ 线性表示，则 $r(\alpha_{1} + \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}) = r(\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{4})$；

(3) 若 $\alpha_{4}$ 不能由 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ 线性表示，则 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ 线性相关；

(4) 若 $\alpha_{4}$ 不能由 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}$ 线性表示，则 $2 \leq r(\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{4}) \leq 3$。

正确命题的个数为（）

A. 1

B. 2

C. 3

D. 4
