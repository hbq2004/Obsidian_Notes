$$
P\{X \ge 0, Y \ge 0\} = \frac{3}{7}, P\{X \ge 0\} = P\{Y \ge 0\} = \frac{4}{7},
$$ 
求下列事件的概率：（1）$A = \{\max\{X,Y\} \ge 0\}$；（2）$B = \{\max\{X,Y\} \ge 0, \min\{X,Y\} < 0\}$.
