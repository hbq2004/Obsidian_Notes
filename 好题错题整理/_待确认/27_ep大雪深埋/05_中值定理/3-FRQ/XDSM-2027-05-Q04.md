# FRQ 第 4 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/XDSM-2027/XDSM-2027-05-Q04_题目.png|题目]]

$$
f'(\xi) \int_{\xi}^{b} g(t) \, dt + g'(\xi) \int_{a}^{\xi} f(t) \, dt = 0.
$$
