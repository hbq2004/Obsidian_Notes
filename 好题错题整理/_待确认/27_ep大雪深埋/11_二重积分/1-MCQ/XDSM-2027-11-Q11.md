# MCQ 第 11 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/XDSM-2027/XDSM-2027-11-Q11_题目.png|题目]]

设 $f(x,y)$ 为连续函数，则 $\displaystyle \int_{1}^{2} dx \int_{0}^{\sqrt{2x - x^{2}}} f(x,y) \, dy = ( )$

(A) $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{1} dy \int_{1}^{1 - \sqrt{1 - y^{2}}} f(x,y) \, dx.$

(B) $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\pi/4} d\theta \int_{1}^{2\sin\theta} f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) \, r \, dr.$

(C) $\displaystyle \int_{1}^{\sqrt{2}} r \, dr \int_{0}^{\arccos\frac{1}{r}} f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) \, d\theta \\[1em] + \int_{\sqrt{2}}^{2} r \, dr \int_{0}^{\arccos\frac{r}{2}} f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) \, d\theta.$

(D) $\displaystyle \int_{0}^{\pi/4} d\theta \int_{0}^{2\cos\theta} f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) \, r \, dr \\[1em] - \int_{0}^{\pi/4} d\theta \int_{0}^{1} f(r\cos\theta, r\sin\theta) \, r \, dr.$
