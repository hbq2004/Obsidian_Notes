# FRQ 第 21 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/XDSM-2027/XDSM-2027-21-Q21_题目.png|题目]]

$$\min \left\{\lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2}, \cdots, \lambda_{n}\right\} \leq \frac{f(x)}{x^{T} x} \leq \max \left\{\lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2}, \cdots, \lambda_{n}\right\}$$

已知 $f(x_1, x_2, x_3) = 3x_1^2 + 4x_2^2 + 3x_3^2 + 2x_1 x_3$。

(1) 求正交矩阵 $Q$ 使 $x = Qy$ 将 $f$ 化为标准形；

(2) 证明 $\min\limits_{x \neq 0} \frac{f(x)}{x^T x} = 2$.
