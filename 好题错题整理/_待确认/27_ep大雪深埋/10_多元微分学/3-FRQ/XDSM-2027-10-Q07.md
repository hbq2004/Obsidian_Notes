# FRQ 第 7 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/XDSM-2027/XDSM-2027-10-Q07_题目.png|题目]]

$\frac{d y}{~d x}=-\frac{g_{1}^{\prime} f_{1}^{\prime}+g_{2}^{\prime}\left(f_{1}^{\prime}+y f_{2}^{\prime}\right)}{x f_{2}^{\prime} g_{2}^{\prime}-f_{1}^{\prime}}$

已知函数 $f(x, y, z) = x^3 y^2 z$ 及方程

$$x + y + z - 3 + e^{-3} = e^{-(x + y + z)}$$

(2) 如果 $z = z(x, y)$ 是由方程 (*) 确定的隐函数，满足 $z(1,1) = 1$，又 $u = f(x, y, z(x, y))$，求 $\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}$
