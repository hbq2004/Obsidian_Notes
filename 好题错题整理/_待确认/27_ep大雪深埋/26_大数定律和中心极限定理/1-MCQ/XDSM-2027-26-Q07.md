$ 三、选择题 (1) 每小题 3 分，共 7 分 $

A. $\lim\limits_{n \to \infty} P\left\{\frac{X_n - n}{\sqrt{n}} \leq x\right\} = \Phi(x)$

B. $\lim\limits_{n \to \infty} P\left\{\frac{X_n - 2n}{\sqrt{n}} \leq x\right\} = \Phi(x)$

C. $\lim\limits_{n \to \infty} P\left\{\frac{2X_n - n}{\sqrt{n}} \leq x\right\} = \Phi(x)$

D. $\lim\limits_{n \to \infty} P\left\{\frac{2X_n - 2n}{\sqrt{n}} \leq x\right\} = \Phi(x)$

答案：C.
