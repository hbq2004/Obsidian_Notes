# MCQ 第 2 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/OJD-202608/OJD-202608-T68FFDF-Q002_题目.png|题目]]

2.设 $f(x,y)$ 是连续函数，则 $\int_{-\sqrt{3}}^{0} ~dx \int_{\frac{1}{3}x^{2}}^{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}} f(x,y) dy =$

(A) $\int_{0}^{1} ~dy \int_{0}^{\sqrt{3y}} f(x,y) dx + \int_{1}^{2} dy \int_{0}^{\sqrt{4-y^{2}}} f(x,y) dx$.

(B) $\int_{0}^{1} dy \int_{-\sqrt{3y}}^{0} f(x,y) dx + \int_{1}^{2} dy \int_{0}^{\sqrt{4-y^{2}}} f(x,y) dx$.

(C) $\int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{5\pi}{6}} ~d\theta \int_{0}^{2} f(r \cos \theta, r \sin \theta) r dr + \int_{\frac{5\pi}{6}}^{\pi} d\theta \int_{0}^{3 \tan \theta \sec \theta} f(r \cos \theta, r \sin \theta) r dr$.

(D) $\int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{2\pi}{3}} d\theta \int_{0}^{2} f(r \cos \theta, r \sin \theta) r dr + \int_{\frac{2\pi}{3}}^{\pi} d\theta \int_{0}^{3 \tan \theta \sec \theta} f(r \cos \theta, r \sin \theta) r dr$.
