数一24-21.(本题满分12分)
已知数列 $\{x_n\},\{y_n\},\{z_n\}$ 满足 $x_0=-1,y_0=0,z_0=2$，且
$$\begin{cases}$$
x_n = -2x_{n-1} + 2z_{n-1}, \\
y_n = -2y_{n-1} - 2z_{n-1}, \\
z_n = -6x_{n-1} - 3y_{n-1} + 3z_{n-1},
\end{cases}$$$$
记 $\alpha_n = \begin{pmatrix} x_n \\ y_n \\ z_n \end{pmatrix}$，写出满足 $\alpha_n = A\alpha_{n-1}$ 的矩阵 $A$，并求 $A^n$ 及 $x_n,y_n,z_n,(n=1,2,\cdots)$.
