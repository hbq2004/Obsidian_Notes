# FIB 第 1 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/27_张宇36讲-题目/L25-Q01_题目.png|题目]]

设$A$为3阶矩阵，$P$为3阶可逆矩阵，且$P^{-1}AP = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$。若$P = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$。

若$P = [\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3], Q = [\alpha_1 + \alpha_2, \alpha_2, \alpha_3]$，则$Q^{-1}AQ = (\ \ )$.

(A) $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$

(B) $\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$

(C) $\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$

(D) $\begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
