$$D_{n} = \begin{vmatrix} 2a & 1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 & 0 \\ a^{2} & 2a & 1 & \cdots & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & a^{2} & 2a & \cdots & 0 & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & 2a & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \cdots & a^{2} & 2a \end{vmatrix} = (n+1)a^{n}.$$
