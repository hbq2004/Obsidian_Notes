(1) $X_1^2$;

(2) $X_2^2 + X_3^2$;

(3) $\frac{(X_2 + X_3)^2}{2}$;

(4) $\frac{\sqrt{2}X_1}{\sqrt{X_2^2 + X_3^2}}$;

(5) $\frac{X_1}{|X_2 - X_3|}$;

(6) $\frac{X_1^2}{(X_2 - X_3)^2}$;

(7) $\frac{X_1 + X_2}{X_3^2 + X_4^2}$;

(8) $\frac{X_1^2}{X_3^2 + X_4^2}$.
