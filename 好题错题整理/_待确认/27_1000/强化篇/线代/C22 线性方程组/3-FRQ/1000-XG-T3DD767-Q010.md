# FRQ 第 10 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/1000-XG/1000-XG-T3DD767-Q010_题目.png|题目]]

$ 10. 设  \alpha_{1}=\left[\begin{array}{lllll} 1, & -2, & 1, & 0, & 0 \end{array}\right]^{T}, \alpha_{2}=\left[\begin{array}{lllll} 1, & -2, & 0, & 1, & 0 \end{array}\right]^{T}, \alpha_{3}=\left[\begin{array}{lllll} 0, & 0, & 1, & -1, & 0 \end{array}\right]^{T}, \alpha_{4}=\left[\begin{array}{lllll} 1, & -2, & 3, & -2, & 0 \end{array}\right]^{T}  是线性方程 $

组

$$\left\{\begin{array}{l} x_{1}+x_{2}+x_{3}+x_{4}+x_{5}=0, \\ 3 x_{1}+2 x_{2}+x_{3}+x_{4}-3 x_{5}=0, \\ x_{2}+2 x_{3}+2 x_{4}+6 x_{5}=0, \\ 5 x_{1}+4 x_{2}+3 x_{3}+3 x_{4}-x_{5}=0 \end{array}\right.$$

（*）的解向量，则 $\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}, \alpha_{4}$ 能否构成方程组（*）的基础解系？若能，说明理由；若不能，请增或减向量，使之成为基础解系.
