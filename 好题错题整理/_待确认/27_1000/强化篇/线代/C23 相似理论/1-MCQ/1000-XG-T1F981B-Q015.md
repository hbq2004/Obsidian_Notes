# MCQ 第 15 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/1000-XG/1000-XG-T1F981B-Q015_题目.png|题目]]

$ 15. 设 2 阶实对称矩阵  A  的特征值为  \lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2} , 且  \lambda_{1} \neq \lambda_{2}, \alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}  分别是  A  的对应于  \lambda_{1}, \lambda_{2}  的单位特征向量, $

则与矩阵 $A + \alpha_{1} \alpha_{1}^{T}$ 相似的对角矩阵为 $(\quad)$.

A. $\left[\begin{array}{cc}\lambda_{1} & 0 \\ 0 & \lambda_{2}\end{array}\right]$

B. $\left[\begin{array}{cc}\lambda_{1}+1 & 0 \\ 0 & \lambda_{2}+1\end{array}\right]$

C. $\left[\begin{array}{cc}\lambda_{1} & 0 \\ 0 & \lambda_{2}+1\end{array}\right]$

D. $\left[\begin{array}{cc}\lambda_{1}+1 & 0 \\ 0 & \lambda_{2}\end{array}\right]$

答案：B
