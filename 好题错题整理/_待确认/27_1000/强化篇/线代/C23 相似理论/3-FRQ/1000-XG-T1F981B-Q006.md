# FRQ 第 6 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/1000-XG/1000-XG-T1F981B-Q006_题目.png|题目]]

$ 6. 设  A, P  均为 3 阶矩阵,  P=\left[\begin{array}{lll}\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}\end{array}\right] , 其中  \alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}  为 3 维列向量组且线性无关, $

$ 若  A\left[\alpha_{1}, \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{3}\right]=\left[3 \alpha_{3}, 2 \alpha_{2}, \alpha_{1}\right] . $

(1) 证明 A 可相似于对角矩阵 A;

(2) 若 $P = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -1 & -1 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$，求可逆矩阵 C，使得 $C^{-1} AC = \Lambda$，并写出 $\Lambda$.
