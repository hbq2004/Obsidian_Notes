# MCQ 第 13 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/1000-GS/1000-GS-TA0FEE5-Q013_题目.png|题目]]

$ 13. 设  f(x, y)  可微,  p(x, y), p_{0}\left(x_{0}, y_{0}\right)  为曲面  z=f(x, y)  上的点, 则( ). $

A. $\lim\limits ＿{p \rightarrow p_{0}} \frac{f(p)+\left[f\left(p_{0}\right)-\left.\operatorname{grad} f\right|＿{p_{0}} \circ \overrightarrow{p_{0} p}\right]}{\left.\overrightarrow{p_{0} p}\right|}=0$

B. $\lim\limits ＿{p \rightarrow p_{0}} \frac{f(p)-\left[f\left(p_{0}\right)-\left.\operatorname{grad} f\right|＿{p_{0}} \cdot \overrightarrow{p_{0} p}\right]}{\left.\overrightarrow{p_{0} p}\right|}=0$

C. $\lim\limits ＿{p \rightarrow p_{0}} \frac{f(p)+\left[f\left(p_{0}\right)+\left.\operatorname{grad} f\right|＿{p_{0}} \cdot \overrightarrow{p_{0} p}\right]}{\left.\overrightarrow{p_{0} p}\right|}=0$

D. $\lim\limits ＿{p \rightarrow p_{0}} \frac{f(p)-\left[f\left(p_{0}\right)+\left.\operatorname{grad} f\right|＿{p_{0}} \cdot \overrightarrow{p_{0} p}\right]^{2}}{\left.\overrightarrow{p_{0} p}\right|}=0$
