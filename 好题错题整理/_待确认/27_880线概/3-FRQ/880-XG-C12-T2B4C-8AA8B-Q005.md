![[_Attachments/题目识别/880-XG/880-XG-C12-T2B4C-8AA8B-Q005_题目.png|题目]]

(5) 设 $A = (\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4)$，$\alpha_1 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}$，$\alpha_2 = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$，$\alpha_3 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$，$\alpha_4 = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ -1 \\ -1 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$。

(I) 证明: $\alpha_1, \alpha_2$ 是 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4$ 的极大线性无关组。

(II) 记 $G = (\alpha_1, \alpha_2)$，求矩阵 $H$，使得 $A = GH$，并求 $A^8$.
