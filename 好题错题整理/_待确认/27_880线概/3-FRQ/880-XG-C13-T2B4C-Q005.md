(5) 设有方程组

$\textcircled{2} \left\{ \begin{array}{l} x_{1} + a x_{2} - x_{3} - x_{4} = -5, \\ b x_{2} - x_{3} - 2 x_{4} = -11, \\ x_{3} - 2 x_{4} = -c + 1. \end{array} \right.$

(I) 求方程组(1)的通解;

(II) 当 $a, b, c$ 为何值时, 方程组(1)与(2)同解.
