(2) 设 $X$ 是连续型随机变量，且 $P\{X \leq a\} = P\{X > b\} = \frac{1}{4}$，令

$X_{1} = \begin{cases} -1, & X \leq a, \\ 1, & X > a, \end{cases} \quad X_{2} = \begin{cases} -1, & X \leq b, \\ 1, & X > b. \end{cases}$

求: (I) $(X_{1}, X_{2})$ 的联合分布及边缘分布;

(III) $Cov\left(X_{1}, X_{2}\right), D\left(X_{1} - X_{2}\right)$.
