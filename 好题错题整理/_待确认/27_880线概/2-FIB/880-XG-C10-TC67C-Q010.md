# FIB 第 10 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/880-XG/880-XG-C10-TC67C-Q010_题目.png|题目]]

(10) 设 3 阶方阵 $A = \left( \alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3 \right)$，$B = \left( 3\alpha_1 - \alpha_2, 3\alpha_2 - 2\alpha_1, 2\alpha_3 - \alpha_1 - 2\alpha_2 \right)$，且 $|B| = 14$，则 $|A| = \＿$.

---

## 答案

![[_Attachments/题目识别/880-答案/880-XG-C10-TC67C-Q010_答案.png|答案]]

## V4 Pro Max 解析

**考点**：列向量的线性组合写成 $B = AM$,则 $|B| = |A|\cdot|M|$,由 $|M|$ 反求 $|A|$。

**解**
$$B = (3\alpha_1 - \alpha_2,\ 3\alpha_2 - 2\alpha_1,\ 2\alpha_3 - \alpha_1 - 2\alpha_2) = (\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)\begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 & -1 \\ -1 & 3 & -2 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 \end{pmatrix} = AM,$$
其中 $M$ 的各列是 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$ 的系数。

$$|B| = |A|\cdot|M|,\qquad |M| = 2\cdot\begin{vmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ -1 & 3 \end{vmatrix} = 2\cdot(9 - 2) = 14.$$
由 $|B| = 14$ 得
$$|A|\cdot14 = 14 \implies |A| = 1.$$

**答案**：$|A| = 1$.

**易错点**：$M$ 的第 3 列是 $(-1, -2, 2)^T$(分别对应 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$ 的系数),别写反;上三角分块 $|M| = 2(9-2)$。
