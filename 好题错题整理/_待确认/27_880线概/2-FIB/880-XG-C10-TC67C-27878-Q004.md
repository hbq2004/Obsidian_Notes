# FIB 第 4 题

(4)设 $A$ 是 3 阶方阵，$\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$ 线性无关，且 $A\alpha_1 = \alpha_1 + \alpha_2$, $A\alpha_2 = \alpha_2 + \alpha_3$, $A\alpha_3 = \alpha_3 + \alpha_1$，则 $|A| =$ \_ .

---

## 答案

![[_Attachments/题目识别/880-答案/880-XG-C10-TC67C-27878-Q004_答案.png|答案]]

## V4 Pro Max 解析

**考点**：$A$ 在线性无关向量组 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$ 下的表示矩阵,由 $AP = PM$ 得 $|A| = |M|$。

**解**
$$A(\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3) = (\alpha_1+\alpha_2,\ \alpha_2+\alpha_3,\ \alpha_3+\alpha_1) = (\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}.$$

记 $P = (\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3)$。因 $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$ 线性无关,$P$ 可逆。由 $AP = PM$ 两边取行列式:
$$|A|\cdot|P| = |P|\cdot|M| \implies |A| = |M|.$$
$$|M| = \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 1\cdot(1\cdot1 - 0\cdot1) - 0 + 1\cdot(1\cdot1 - 1\cdot0) = 1 + 1 = 2.$$

**答案**：$|A| = 2$.

**易错点**：表示矩阵 $M$ 的第 2 列是 $A\alpha_2 = \alpha_2 + \alpha_3$ 的坐标 $(0,1,1)^T$;$\alpha_i$ 线性无关保证 $P$ 可逆,才能约去 $|P|$。
