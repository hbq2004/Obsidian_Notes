# FRQ 第 35 题

计算下列极限:

$$
\begin{aligned}
& \underset{n \rightarrow \infty}{\lim} \sqrt{(n^{2}+1)(n^{2}+2^{2})} \cdots (n^{2}+n^{2}) \\
& (I) \lim\limits_{n \rightarrow \infty} \sum\limits_{k=1}^{n} \frac{k}{n^{2}} \arctan \sqrt{\frac{k}{n}}; \\
& (II) \lim\limits_{n \rightarrow \infty} \frac{\sqrt{(n^{2}+1)(n^{2}+2^{2})} \cdots (n^{2}+n^{2})}{n^{2}}
\end{aligned}
$$
