# MCQ 第 4 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/900-XG/900-XG-PS-C06-A-Q004_题目.png|题目]]

设总体 $X \sim N(\mu, \sigma^2)$ ($\sigma > 0$)，$X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_{2n}$ ($n > 2$) 为来自该总体的简单随机样本。记 $\overline{X}_1 = \frac{1}{n} \sum\limits_{i=1}^n X_i$，$\overline{X}_2 = \frac{1}{n} \sum\limits_{j=n+1}^{2n} X_j$，则下列结论中，正确的是（）

A. $\frac{\overline{X}_1 - \overline{X}_2}{\sigma} \sim N(0, 1)$.

B. $\sum\limits_{i=1}^n \frac{\left(X_i - X_{n+i}\right)^2}{\sigma^2} \sim \chi^2(n)$.

C. $\frac{n\left(\overline{X}_2 - \mu\right)}{\sqrt{\sum\limits_{i=1}^n \left(X_i - \mu\right)^2}} \sim t(n)$.

D. $\frac{\sum\limits_{i=1}^n \left(X_i - \overline{X}_1\right)^2}{\sum\limits_{j=n+1}^{2n} \left(X_j - \overline{X}_2\right)^2} \sim F(n, n)$.
