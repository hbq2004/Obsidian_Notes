# MCQ 第 3 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/900-XG/900-XG-PS-C06-B-Q003_题目.png|题目]]

设 $X_1, X_2, \cdots, X_n$ 为来自总体 $X \sim N(\mu_1, \sigma_1^2)$ ($\sigma_1 > 0$) 的简单随机样本, $Y_1, Y_2, \cdots, Y_m$ 为来自总体 $Y \sim N(\mu_2, \sigma_2^2)$ ($\sigma_2 > 0$) 的简单随机样本, 样本方差分别为 $S_1^2, S_2^2$, 则下列命题中, 正确的是()

A. 若 $E(S_1^2) = E(S_2^2)$, 则 $m = n$.

B. 若 $D(S_1^2) = \frac{\sigma_1^4}{49}$, $D(S_2^2) = \frac{\sigma_2^4}{50}$, 则 $n = 49, m = 50$.

C. 若 $\sigma_1^2 = \sigma_2^2$, 则 $\frac{\sqrt{n}(\overline{X} - \mu_1)}{S_2} \sim t(n-1)$.

D. 若 $\sigma_1^2 = \sigma_2^2$, 则 $\frac{S_1^2}{S_2^2} \sim F(n-1, m-1)$.
