# MCQ 第 3 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/900-XG/900-XG-LA-C02-A-Q003_题目.png|题目]]

设 $A$ 为 3 阶矩阵, $P$ 为 3 阶可逆矩阵, 且 $P^{-1}AP = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$. 若 $P = \begin{pmatrix} \alpha_1 & \alpha_2 & \alpha_3 \end{pmatrix}$，$Q = \begin{pmatrix} \alpha_1 + \alpha_2, \alpha_2 + \alpha_3, \alpha_3 \end{pmatrix}$，则 $Q^{-1}AQ = ( )$

A. $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

B. $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

C. $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 2 & 0 \\ -1 & 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.

D. $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & -1 \\ 0 & 2 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$.
