① $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{f(x)}{\sqrt{1+F^{2}(x)}} dx$

② $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{f(x)}{\sqrt{1-F^{2}(x)}} dx$

③ $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{f(x)}{1+F^{2}(x)} dx$

④ $\int_{0}^{+\infty} \frac{f(x)}{1-F^{2}(x)} dx$

A. 0.

B. 1.

C. 2.

D. 3.
