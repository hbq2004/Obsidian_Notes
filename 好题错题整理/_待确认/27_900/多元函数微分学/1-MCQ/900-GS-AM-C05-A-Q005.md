# MCQ 第 5 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/900-GS/900-GS-AM-C05-A-Q005_题目.png|题目]]

已知二元函数 $f(x,y)$ 存在一阶偏导数.对于任意 $y$, 若 $|x_1|>|x_2|$, 则 $f(x_1,y)>f(x_2,y)$; 对于任意 $x$, 若 $|y_1>|y_2|$, 则 $f(x,y_1)<f(x,y_2)$.下列结论中, 正确的是()

A. $\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right|_{(-1,1)}\geqslant\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right|_{(1,-1)}$

B. $\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,-1)}\geqslant\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right|_{(-1,1)}$

C. $\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right|_{(1,1)}\geqslant\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right|_{(-1,-1)}$

D. $\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right|_{(-1,-1)}\geqslant\left.\frac{\partial f}{\partial y}\right|_{(1,1)}$
