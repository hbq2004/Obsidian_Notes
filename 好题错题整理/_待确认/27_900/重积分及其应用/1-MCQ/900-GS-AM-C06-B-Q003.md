# MCQ 第 3 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/900-GS/900-GS-AM-C06-B-Q003_题目.png|题目]]

设函数 $f(x, y)$ 连续, $a > 0$, 则 $\int_{-\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}}^{\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}} dx \int_{|x|}^{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}} f(x, y) dy = ( )$

A. $2 \int_{0}^{\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}} dx \int_{x}^{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}} f(x, y) dy$.

B. $\int_{0}^{-\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}} dx \int_{-x}^{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}} f(x, y) dy + \int_{0}^{\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}} dx \int_{x}^{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}} f(x, y) dy$.

C. $2 \int_{0}^{\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}} dy \int_{0}^{y} f(x, y) dx + 2 \int_{0}^{\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}} dy \int_{0}^{\sqrt{a^2 - y^2}} f(x, y) dx$.

D. $\int_{0}^{\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}} dy \int_{-y}^{y} f(x, y) dx + \int_{0}^{\frac{a}{\sqrt{2}}} dy \int_{-\sqrt{a^2 - y^2}}^{\sqrt{a^2 - y^2}} f(x, y) dx$.
