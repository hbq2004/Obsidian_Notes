# MCQ 第 4 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/900-GS/900-GS-AM-C06-B-Q004_题目.png|题目]]

设函数 $f(x, y)$ 连续，且为关于 $x$ 的偶函数，区域 $D$ 由曲线 $y = \frac{1}{4} x^2$，$x^2 + (y-1)^2 = 1$ 以及直线 $y = 1$ 围成，则 $\iint_D f(x, y) \, dx \, dy = ( )$

A. $\int_0^1 dy \int_{-\sqrt{y}}^{-2\sqrt{y}} f(x, y) \, dx + \int_0^1 dy \int_{\sqrt{1-(y-1)^2}}^{\sqrt{2\sqrt{y}}} f(x, y) \, dx$.

B. $2 \left[ \int_0^1 dx \int_{\frac{1}{4} x^2}^{1+\sqrt{1-x^2}} f(x, y) \, dy + \int_0^1 dx \int_{\frac{1}{4} x^2}^{1} f(x, y) \, dy \right]$.

C. $2 \left[ \int_0^{\arctan 2} d\theta \int_{2\sin\theta}^{4\tan\theta \sec \theta} f(r \cos \theta, r \sin \theta) \, r \, dr + \int_{\arctan 2}^{\frac{\pi}{\sin \theta}} d\theta \int_{2\sin \theta}^{\frac{1}{\sin \theta}} f(r \cos \theta, r \sin \theta) \, r \, dr \right]$.

D. $2 \left[ \int_0^{\arctan \frac{1}{2}} d\theta \int_{2\sin \theta}^{4\tan \theta \sec \theta} f(r \cos \theta, r \sin \theta) \, r \, dr + \int_{\arctan \frac{1}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}} d\theta \int_{2\sin \theta}^{\frac{1}{\sin \theta}} f(r \cos \theta, r \sin \theta) \, r \, dr \right]$.
