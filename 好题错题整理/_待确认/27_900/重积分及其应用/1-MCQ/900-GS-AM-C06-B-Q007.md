A. $\frac{v}{u}f(\sqrt{u})$.
B. $\frac{v}{u^{2}}f(\sqrt{u})$.
C. $\frac{v}{u}f(u)$.
D. $\frac{v}{u^{2}}f(u)$.

$\begin{aligned} & A. \frac{v}{u} f(\sqrt{u}) . \\ & B. \frac{v}{u^{2}} f(\sqrt{u}) . \\ & C. \frac{v}{u} f(u) . \\ & D. \frac{v}{u^{2}} f(u) . \end{aligned}$
