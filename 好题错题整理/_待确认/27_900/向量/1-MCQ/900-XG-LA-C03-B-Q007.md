# MCQ 第 7 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/900-XG/900-XG-LA-C03-B-Q007_题目.png|题目]]

设向量 $\alpha_1 = (1,1,0,0)^T$, $\alpha_2 = (1,0,1,0)^T$, $\alpha_3 = (1,0,0,1)^T$, $\alpha_4 = (\lambda,1,x,x^2)^T$, $\alpha_5 = \left(\lambda,1,\frac{1}{x},\frac{1}{x^2}\right)^T$. 若对所有 $x \neq 0$，向量组 $I: \alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4$ 与向量组 $II: \alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_5$ 恒等价，则 $\lambda$ 的取值范围是（）

A. $\lambda < \frac{3}{4}$.
B. $\lambda < \frac{3}{4}$ 或 $\lambda = 1$.
C. $\lambda > \frac{5}{4}$.
D. $\lambda > \frac{5}{4}$ 或 $\lambda = 1$.
