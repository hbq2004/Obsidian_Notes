# MCQ 第 278 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/YW-2026/YW-2026-TE732A7-Q278_题目.png|题目]]

设 $A$，$B$ 均为 $n$ 阶矩阵，$E$ 是 $n$ 阶单位矩阵，$O$ 是 $n$ 阶零矩阵，分析以下分块矩阵的秩，错误的等式是（）

A. $r\left(\begin{array}{cc}A & O \\ BA & B\end{array}\right) = r\left(\begin{array}{cc}B & AB \\ O & A\end{array}\right)$

B. $r\left(\begin{array}{cc}O & A \\ B & BA\end{array}\right) = r\left(\begin{array}{cc}O & B \\ A & AB\end{array}\right)$

C. $r\left(\begin{array}{cc}A & B \\ B & E\end{array}\right) = n + r\left(A - B^2\right)$

D. $r\left(\begin{array}{cc}A^{T}A & O \\ A & B\end{array}\right) = r\left(\begin{array}{cc}B & O \\ AA^{T} & A\end{array}\right)$

因此，正确答案为 D.
