# FRQ 第 214 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/YW-2026/YW-2026-TE732A7-Q214_题目.png|题目]]

设 $A$ 为 $n$ 阶矩阵，分析以下矩阵秩的大小关系：

$$r(A) \quad r\left(A^{T}\right) \quad r\left(A A^{T}\right) \quad r\left(A^{T} A\right)$$

$$r\left[\begin{array}{l} A \\ A^{T} \end{array}\right], \quad r\left[\begin{array}{l} A A^{T} \\ A^{T} \end{array}\right], \quad r\left[\begin{array}{l} A^{T} A \\ A^{T} \end{array}\right] ; $$

$$r\left[\begin{array}{l} A^{T} \\ A \end{array}\right], \quad r\left[\begin{array}{l} A A^{T} \\ A \end{array}\right], \quad r\left[\begin{array}{l} A^{T} A \\ A \end{array}\right] ; $$

$$r\left[A, A^{T}\right], \quad r\left[A A^{T}, A^{T}\right], \quad r\left[A^{T} A, A^{T}\right]$$

$$r\left[A^{T}, A\right], \quad r\left[A A^{T}, A\right], \quad r\left[A^{T} A, A\right]$$
