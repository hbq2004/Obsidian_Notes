# MCQ 第 241 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/YW-2026/YW-2026-T4E4366-Q241_题目.png|题目]]

设 $n$ 阶实对称矩阵 $A$，$B$ 均可逆，则下列说法中，正确的是()

A. 矩阵 $\left( \begin{array}{cc} E & BA \\ AB & O \end{array} \right)$ 与 $\left( \begin{array}{cc} E & O \\ O & O \end{array} \right)$ 合同.

B. 矩阵 $\left( \begin{array}{cc} E & BA \\ AB & O \end{array} \right)$ 与 $\left( \begin{array}{cc} E & O \\ O & E \end{array} \right)$ 合同.

C. 矩阵 $\left( \begin{array}{cc} E & BA \\ AB & -E \end{array} \right)$ 与 $\left( \begin{array}{cc} E & O \\ O & E \end{array} \right)$ 合同.

D. 矩阵 $\left( \begin{array}{cc} E & BA \\ AB & -E \end{array} \right)$ 与 $\left( \begin{array}{cc} E & O \\ O & -E \end{array} \right)$ 合同.
