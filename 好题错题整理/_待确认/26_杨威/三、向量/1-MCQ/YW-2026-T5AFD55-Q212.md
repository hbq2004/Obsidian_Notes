# MCQ 第 212 题

![[_Attachments/题目识别/YW-2026/YW-2026-T5AFD55-Q212_题目.png|题目]]

(8) 设 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4$ 是 $n$ 维非零向量,则下列结论正确的是().

A. 若 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2$ 线性相关, $\alpha_3, \alpha_4$ 线性相关, 则 $\alpha_1 + \alpha_3, \alpha_2 + \alpha_4$ 线性相关.

B. 若 $\alpha_4$ 不能由 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$ 线性表示, 则 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3$ 线性相关.

C. 设 $A\beta_1 = \alpha_1, A\beta_2 = \alpha_1 + \alpha_2, A\beta_3 = \alpha_1 + \alpha_3, A\beta_4 = \alpha_1 + \alpha_4$, 其中 $n$ 阶矩阵 $A$ 可逆, 若 $\beta_1, \beta_2, \beta_3, \beta_4$ 线性无关, 则 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4$ 线性无关.

D. 设 $A = (\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4)$, 若 $\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \alpha_3, \alpha_4$ 线性无关, 则矩阵 $AA^T$ 正定.
